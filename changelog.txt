Date: 2026-10-09
  Gui:
    - Pack indicators now distinguish In stock, Out of stock, and Not researched, with Stock not monitored when no inventory reading is available.
    - Stock indicators reflect the monitored lab's latest inventory check independently of manual pack settings and the Dynamic grace period.

Version: 1.1.1
Date: 2026-10-08
  Bugfixes:
    - Switching automatic research now removes the previous automatic choice instead of leaving it queued.
    - Player-queued technologies retain their order and take priority over automatic research.
    - Research limits only remove automatically queued work; manually queued research is preserved.
  Changes:
    - Existing queues from older versions are treated as player-owned because their origin was not recorded.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.1.0
Date: 2026-10-07
  Features:
    - Added On, Off, and Dynamic modes for research packs.
    - Added Monitor this lab and Stop monitoring controls beside the lab GUI, with one monitored lab per force.
    - Monitoring a different lab replaces the previous source; manual On and Off pack choices remain independent of monitoring.
    - Dynamic packs follow the monitored lab's inventory and affect research eligibility and list sorting.
    - Added editable pack check interval and empty pack grace period settings, defaulting to 10 and 60 seconds.
  Gui:
    - Aligned the timing settings labels and inputs in shared columns.
    - Replaced pack mode radio buttons with compact On / Off / Dynamic dropdowns.
    - Packs without a saved choice default to On after their unlocking research, and Off beforehand; explicit choices are retained.
    - Made the lab monitoring panel compact and collapsible, remembering expansion separately for each player.
    - Dynamic mode is unavailable without a monitored lab, with a tooltip explaining how to enable it.
    - Pack icons indicate effective availability, and monitoring changes synchronize across players.
    - Removing a monitored lab clears monitoring while retaining Dynamic selections and research limits.
  Changes:
    - Removed the per-player default queued and default blacklisted technology settings.
    - Added the GitHub repository link to the mod metadata.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed a crash when opening a lab before any panel expansion preference had been saved.
    - Fixed a configuration-change crash from applying button-only grayscale styling to pack images; availability now uses status indicators.
    - Fixed a reserved GUI field name causing a crash when creating the monitoring settings.
  Locale:
    - Added English and Russian translations for monitoring, pack modes, and timing settings.
